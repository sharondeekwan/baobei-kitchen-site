Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 14, 2026

Overview

BaoBei Kitchen is a recipe, meal-planning and grocery app made by BaoBei Labs. This policy explains what the app stores, where it is stored, and what happens when you share something.

Where your data lives

Everything you create in BaoBei Kitchen — recipes, meal plans, grocery lists, photos and cook logs — is stored on your device.

If you are signed in to iCloud, the same data also syncs to your own iCloud account, so it appears on your other Apple devices. That copy belongs to you. It is held by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy, and BaoBei Labs cannot read it.

If you are not signed in to iCloud, the app works entirely on your device and nothing syncs.

Sharing a recipe by link

When you tap Share Recipe, the app uploads that one recipe — and one of its photos, if it has one — to our storage on Google Firebase, so that the person you send it to can open it. This only happens when you choose to share.

  • The file is identified by a random code. It is not listed, indexed or searchable.
  • It is deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Anyone who has the link can open it while it lasts, so only send it to people you intend to.

We do not read these files, and nothing else in your library is uploaded.

What we do not collect

  • We have no user accounts and no logins.
  • We do not collect your name, email address or contact details.
  • We do not read your recipes, meal plans, grocery lists or photos.
  • We do not use cookies, advertising identifiers, or serve ads.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with data brokers.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.

Permissions

The app may ask for the following device permissions:

  • Camera — to take photos of your recipes. Photos stay on your device and in your own iCloud, and are included only if you choose to share that recipe.
  • Photo Library — to pick existing photos for your recipes. Selected photos are copied into the app’s own storage.
  • Reminders — to export your grocery list to the Apple Reminders app on your device.
  • Notifications — to send meal-planning reminders. These are scheduled on your device and do not involve a server.

Analytics and crash reporting

BaoBei Kitchen uses Google Firebase for crash reporting (Crashlytics) and basic usage analytics. This helps us find and fix bugs, and see which features are used so we can improve the app.

Firebase receives:

  • Crash reports — stack traces, device model, OS version, and app state at the moment of a crash. No recipe content and no personal data.
  • Usage events — anonymous events such as “a recipe was created” or “the grocery list was cleared”. We never record which recipe, what ingredients, or anything you typed.
  • Device information — device model, operating system version, and app version, so we can prioritise fixes for the most common devices.

This data is not linked to your identity. Firebase data is processed by Google in accordance with Google’s Firebase privacy policy. No other third-party services, advertising SDKs or tracking tools are used.

Keeping your data safe

Uploads travel over encrypted connections. Files on our storage are protected by rules limiting their size and type, and by Firebase App Check, which confirms that an upload came from a genuine copy of the app rather than a script. Data in iCloud is protected by Apple’s encryption and by your Apple Account.

Deleting your data

You can delete any recipe, meal plan entry, grocery item or cook log in the app at any time. Deleting the app removes the copy on your device. Data you have synced to your own iCloud is removed by deleting it in the app, or by removing the app’s data from your iCloud settings. Shared recipe links expire on their own after 30 days.

Children’s privacy

BaoBei Kitchen is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version is posted on this page with a new effective date.
